Full description:
Verimots is a native Android app: a French word checker, a rack search, and a daily letter challenge.

Offline first
The word list is bundled: more than 400,000 unaccented forms from 2 to 15 letters. Check a word, search a rack (letters + blanks), and play the daily challenge with no internet connection.

Automatic definitions
A connection is needed only for automatic definitions (Wiktionnaire) and to share a word or a challenge.

Daily challenge and optional competition
Play a new letter rack every day. Stay anonymous, or sign in with Google to appear on the daily leaderboard. First score of the day counts.

Community-maintained list
The list is not the official Larousse dictionary. Verimots is not affiliated with Mattel or any federation.

No ads. The checker and the anonymous challenge work without an account.
