Verimots is a companion for word-game players: check a word in a second, find the top play on a rack, practise anagrams. The word lists live inside the app and work offline.

CHECK A WORD
• A challenge at the table? Type the word: playable or not, its value in points and, when online, its definition (Wiktionary).
• English lists: a community list following CSW (257,218 words) or WGPO WOW24 (195,383), from 2 to 15 letters.
• Advanced mode: rack search with blanks (?), words that start with, end with or contain given letters.
• Favourites and history.

FIND THE TOP PLAY
• Find a word: a rack of letters, look for the word that scores most. Your score is your percentage of the best play, then the rack’s best words appear with their definitions.
• Points follow the tile values, with a 50-point bonus for a seven-letter word.

PRACTISE
• Combinations: All, 7 letters, 8 letters, 7 + 1, Blank, Hard letters (J, K, Q, W, X, Y, Z) and Short words.
• Short Words: every day, 10 two-letter and 12 three-letter words to revise, with their definitions, plus a challenge mode.
• Review: a rack where the best word got away, or a combination you did not finish, is saved for you. Find the words again without help: it comes back the next day, then in 3, 7 and 21 days. Hints: first letter, then the definition.
• Beginner or Confirmed level.

COMPETE
• Bingo: seven letters, one word, the highest score wins. Weekly leaderboard.
• Leaderboards by category, language and period: day, 3 days, week, 7 days, 30 days, all time.
• Optional Google account, to keep your profile and rankings on the web and on your phone.

FOUR LANGUAGES
English, French (407,128 words), Spanish (RLA-ES, international or North American tiles, with CH, LL and RR as single tiles) and Catalan (DISC).

NO ADS
Free, no ads, no in-app purchases. The internet is only used for definitions, leaderboards, your account and sharing; everything else works offline.

GOOD TO KNOW
The lists are community lists: they are not the official publications and may differ from them. Verimots is an independent project, not affiliated with any publisher, federation, Mattel or Hasbro.
