Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026.

Verimots is a French, English and Spanish word checker and letter game published by pfa87 (s.pfa87.cc, pfanokif@gmail.com). Web and Android app (cc.pfa87.verimots). No ads, no in-app purchases, no sale of personal data.

Version française · Español · Dictionaries · Support

Controller

pfa87, pfanokif@gmail.com. The service is served at s.pfa87.cc.

No account required

Word check, rack search, the anonymous challenge and training work without an account. Google sign-in is optional and is only used for the weekly leaderboard and synced history.

Data on the device

  • Local word history, the 0–100 score chart and training statistics.
  • Language, game mode and interface preferences.
  • A cache of up to 80 definitions, retained for at most seven days.
  • A session token if you are signed in.

Clear site or app storage to delete this data.

Data sent to s.pfa87.cc

Anonymous challenge. If you submit a challenge online, only the percentage (0–100) is sent for the public average. No name, email, advertising ID or played word.

Competitive mode (optional, after Google sign-in). Google sends us:

  • your Google account id (sub);
  • display name;
  • profile photo URL.

We use these for the weekly board (name as pseudo), synced history, and stats (streak, best score). The sub is not shown publicly.

Definitions. When online, the looked-up word is sent to our proxy and then to the matching Wiktionary edition.

Leaderboard. A ranked score may include percentage, optional word, trail date and display name.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no ad tracking, no sale of data.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location or files.
  • We never receive your Google password.

Processors

  • Google — Sign in with Google. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Wikimedia / Wiktionary — definitions when you are online.
  • Cloudflare — HTTPS front for s.pfa87.cc.

Retention

  • Anonymous percentages: stored in aggregate, not tied to a person.
  • Competitive account and history: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Session cookie / token: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

Your rights

To access, export or delete Google-linked data, email pfanokif@gmail.com. We handle deletion requests in a reasonable time. Sign out in the app and clear app data to remove the local copy.

Children

Verimots is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Android permission

Internet only: definitions, public average, Google sign-in, leaderboard, cloud history. The word list, rack and local challenge work offline.

Changes

We will update this page if the processing changes. The date above is the source of truth.
