Available lists

Last updated: 21 August 2026.

Verimots checks a word against the list you selected, not “the” official federation lexicon. The active list and version appear under the title, on the verdict (playable / not in the list) and in the word count. Tap that name in the app to switch lists: Settings.

Français · Español · Open Verimots

FR · ODS9

Community list following ODS (the usual French letter-game reference), edition ODS9. About 407,000 forms of 2 to 15 letters, no accents. Not the Larousse book. Verimots is not affiliated with FFJ, FISF or Mattel.

Use ODS9

EN · CSW · YAWL

Closest public list following Collins Scrabble Words (CSW), the international English lexicon. About 257,000 forms. The redistributable source is YAWL (Yet Another Word List, public domain).

This is not official CSW (HarperCollins copyright). Recent CSW words may be missing (GLAMP is not in YAWL) and YAWL includes forms that are not in CSW. For a Collins tournament this is an approximation, not an adjudicator.

Use CSW · YAWL

EN · WGPO WOW24

Community list following WGPO Official Words 2024 (Word Game Players Organization), in force since 31 March 2024. About 195,000 forms. This is the WGPO club lexicon in the United States, distinct from CSW and from NASPA NWL.

Source: the alphabetical file published by WGPO. Verimots is not affiliated with WGPO, NASPA, Mattel or Hasbro. New English sessions default to WOW24.

Use WGPO WOW24

ES · RLA-ES 2.9

Community list following RLA-ES 2.9 (Mozilla Public License 1.1). About 607,000 forms, Ñ kept, stress marks removed. Not the official FILE / FISE dictionary.

Use RLA-ES

What Verimots does not ship

  • Official CSW (Collins / HarperCollins) — not redistributable.
  • NASPA NWL / OWL.
  • The Larousse ODS book as a product.
